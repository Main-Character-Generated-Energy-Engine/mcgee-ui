# Camera screen copy proposals

Five complete alternatives for each narrator's camera consent screen. Each set replaces the current shared heading, supporting text, camera access sentence, and primary button. The camera sentence says what the app needs without implying permission has already been granted. These are proposals only; none is wired into the UI.

## Morgan — personal story

### 1. The first scene
- **Heading:** Every story begins somewhere.
- **Supporting text:** Morgan is ready to tell yours. Step into the frame, and let the first scene find you.
- **Camera note:** Camera access lets your surroundings become part of the story. Your browser may ask for permission.
- **Button:** Begin my story

### 2. A small beginning
- **Heading:** A small moment can change a story.
- **Supporting text:** Morgan will follow the details as they unfold, one moment at a time.
- **Camera note:** To begin, let the app see your camera. Your browser may ask you to allow access.
- **Button:** Start the story

### 3. The scene is yours
- **Heading:** The scene is yours.
- **Supporting text:** No script to follow. Just the ordinary moments Morgan can turn into a story.
- **Camera note:** The story uses your camera view while you're here. Allow access if your browser asks.
- **Button:** Step into the story

### 4. Ready when you are
- **Heading:** Your story is ready when you are.
- **Supporting text:** Morgan has the opening. The next part starts with whatever appears in frame.
- **Camera note:** We need camera access to continue. Your browser may ask for permission.
- **Button:** Continue my story

### 5. An open ending
- **Heading:** The story is still unfolding.
- **Supporting text:** Morgan will take the first visible detail and see where it leads.
- **Camera note:** Turn on your camera to give the narration a scene to follow. Allow access if prompted.
- **Button:** Let the story begin
