MCgEe Design System

Three colours, one law: purple is who the product is, dark grey is where the work happens, gold means the narrator is speaking. Nothing else may wear gold.

Version 1.0 #ff8f1c · #4f2370 · #1e1e1e

The colour law

MCgEe watches and speaks. The palette encodes that: a user must be able to tell, from across the room and with the audio muted, whether the engine is capturing them. Gold is therefore a state, not a decoration.

Live

Capturing frames and narrating. Gold #ff8f1c, breathing pulse on the 4s cadence. The only condition in which gold appears in the interface.

Standby

Devices armed, nothing captured, nothing sent. Neutral #a3a3a3. Static.

Off air

Idle or disconnected. Purple #724f8d. This is also the brand's resting state everywhere outside the app.

Hard rule. Gold is reserved for the live pipeline and the single primary action that starts it. It is not used for links, headings, decorative gradients, or hover states on unrelated controls. If gold appears while nothing is being captured, the interface is lying about privacy.

Never place purple #4f2370 and dark grey #1e1e1e in direct foreground/background contact. Their contrast ratio is 1.43:1 — effectively invisible. They coexist only as separate zones with a light or gold border between them.

Type

Three faces, three jobs. The split is not stylistic: the wordmark, the interface and the generated narration are three different speakers, and one of them reads out numbers that change every four seconds. Poppins is the logo face, confirmed in the source file, and it leads the system — but it does not run the interface.

Logo-adjacent. 20px and above only.
Start episode
UI · Archivo 400–650

Narrator style · Latency 1.4s · 00:04:12 captured
Narration · Newsreader 350 italic
Generated script, transcript, captions.

He opens his code editor, hesitates on line 42, then commits to a refactor that will change everything.

Why Poppins does not run the interface. Poppins has no tabular-figure feature. Timecode, latency and duration readouts update every few seconds, and proportional digits make them shift horizontally on every update — a live instrument that visibly twitches. Poppins also pairs a small x-height with wide circular forms, which loses density at 12–14px in an app that sits beside a code editor. Archivo carries real tabular figures and squarish narrow forms, and it is the same class of grotesque as the logo tagline. Poppins is the brand's voice; Archivo is its instrument panel.

Poppins runs tight: −.035em at 49px, −.025em at 31px, −.015em at 25px. Untracked Poppins at display size reads as a default template.

Do

  • Set font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums globally on the UI face so every readout is fixed-width.
  • Cap prose at 62 characters; cap narration at 48, so one spoken line fits one visual breath.
  • Keep Poppins above 20px. Below that it is a liability, not a brand asset.

Don't

  • No Poppins in tables, forms, timecode or any figure that updates.
  • No uppercase form labels. Uppercase exists only in on-air badges (LIVE, REC, STANDBY) and the logo tagline.
  • No monospace for data. Tabular Archivo does the job without the terminal cosplay.
  • Don't set narration in either sans. The serif italic is how users tell the machine's voice from the machine's controls.

Shape & space

The logo is built from two circles inside two trapezoids: lenses and bodies. The system inherits that split — round means state or transport, square means capture.

RadiusValueApplied to
--r-frame0Viewfinder, screen preview, recorded still. Capture surfaces have hard corners; they are what the camera sees.
--r-panel3pxPanels, cards, inputs, standard buttons.
--r-pill999pxState badges, transport controls, device toggles. Anything round is pressable or alive.

Borders carry information, not decoration: 1px #2c2c2c separates content of equal weight; 2px appears only where the live pipeline is active, and then in gold. Elevation is expressed by surface lightening (#1e1e1e → #262626), never by shadow — the app sits over video and dark shadows disappear.

Space runs on a 4px base: 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48 · 64 · 96. Section rhythm is 48px inside the app, 96px in marketing.

Motion

One orchestrated moment, tied to the machine's actual behaviour. MCgEe captures every 3–5 seconds; the live indicator breathes on a 4-second cycle so the pulse is a truthful readout of cadence, not ambience.

TokenDurationApplied to
--t-instant90msPress feedback, toggles
--t-quick160msHover, focus, colour changes
--t-considered280msPanel open, narration line entering
--pulse-cycle4sLive indicator only

Nothing else animates on load. New narration lines fade in over 280ms and hold; they never slide, and the transcript does not auto-scroll past the current line without the user's action. prefers-reduced-motion replaces the pulse with a static gold dot and disables all transitions — the state stays legible without movement.

Voice

Interface voice: plain, present tense, sentence case, and literal about capture. The interface never dramatises; the narrator does that. Actions keep their name end to end — "Start episode" produces "Episode started" and "Stop episode".

Narrator voice: third person, cinematic, 1–2 sentences, never explanatory. It describes what is visible and does not claim to know intent.

Interface says

  • "Capturing screen and camera."
  • "Narration is muted. Capture continues."
  • "Webcam is in use by another app."

Interface never says

  • "Oops! Something went wrong."
  • "Unleash your main character energy."
  • "Your story is being magically crafted…"
Privacy is a voice requirement. Any copy about capture states plainly what is being recorded, where it is processed, and how to stop it. This is the one place where the product is allowed to be boring.

Logo

The wordmark is Poppins, confirmed in the Canva source (set at 431px). The exported SVG and PNG are fully outlined — 58 paths, no text nodes — so the lockup is treated as a fixed asset and never re-set in live type. The tagline is a separate text element and renders considerably narrower and more squared than Poppins caps; confirm its face in Canva before it is reproduced anywhere outside the lockup.

Two lockups exist and they are not interchangeable.

LockupConstructionUse on
Purple-groundGold wordmark with white outline, white binocular bodies, gold tagline#4f2370 and #3b1a54 only

Do

  • Keep clear space equal to the height of the lowercase g bowl on all sides.
  • Drop the tagline below 180px of lockup width; the binoculars survive, the 12px caps do not.
  • Use the two lenses alone as the app icon and favicon.

Don't

  • No gold wordmark on white or light grey — 2.28:1, illegible.
  • No purple wordmark on #1e1e1e — 1.43:1, invisible.
  • Don't place the lockup over live video or a screenshot; put it on a flat brand surface.
  • Don't animate the lenses in the logo. The pulse belongs to the live state, and reusing it dilutes the signal.
